Estrella privacy policy

Updated October 31, 2026.

Estrella is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Mark Hilton. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Estrella accesses

With the owner's permission, Estrella reads his Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Contacts and Google Docs, and with his approval sends email, adds or removes calendar events, and archives or marks email as read. It never deletes email.

Where the data goes

Data from Google is kept on the owner's own computer. To write summaries and drafts, parts of it are sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which processes it under Anthropic's commercial terms. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Google user data

Estrella's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Estrella at any time in Estrella or at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete its data from his computer.
